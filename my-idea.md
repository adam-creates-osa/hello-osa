# My idea
- What it is:An app where you can learn more about endangered animal species and geography. With this app, you can learn about the animals and their conservation status (recently extinct and threatened only), as well as the country or continent they live in, and facts about these places (history, flag, traditions, currency, civilizations, etc.). The app will have a special focus on threatened and near threatened animals in Osa and Costa Rica. You can also learn what is destroying the habitats of these animals and what we can do to save the ones just barely hanging on (like the Fernandina Island Tortoise or the Yangtze Softshell Island Tortoise or the Vaquita etc.). The description of facts can be long or short depending on the country. Each animal and country/continent can represent a specially designed card inside the app, which could be printed, collected and/or traded. Aside from offering info, the app will be a world map game where you can play trivia to guess which animal or country/continent is shown. Perhaps the app could include simulations of the habitats inside a country and actions to protect the animals. 
- Who it's for: Tourists, newcomers and locals in  countries around the world. The app will be specially designed for children and students, but anyone can use it.
- Their problem: Many people living in or visiting these countries often do not know about the local habitats and the risks faced by the animals that they come to see. They need easily fun and easily accessible ways to learn more about these places and animals. Our goal is to encourage specially children and students to learn and have fun playing with this app.
- One person I can talk to about it: my parents and my sister. They have traveled around the world and can help me get ideas to improve the app. We are priviledged to live now in one of the most exotic countries in the world: Costa Rica.
